## FM27 Chronicle 0.1.0 — first release

Your FM27 career, on record. Chronicle reads your save live from the running game and builds the season review
FM27 itself does not have. It only reads; it never changes your save.

### What is in it
- **Season review**: position, points, record, goals, win rate, league position after every matchday, form,
  highlights, results per competition, goals by month and how far you got in every cup.
- **Matches** and a **league table** for every season of your save, straight from the game.
- **Career**: trophy cabinet, season by season, all-time records, favourite opponent and bogey team. It follows
  you as a manager: change clubs and your history stays.
- **Squad and finances** recorded every game week from the moment you start using the app.
- **Share card**: your season or your whole career as a PNG.

### Tested
- **Mac (Apple silicon, Steam build of 17 November 2026): read live** on a save of three seasons (179 matches,
  three league tables, seven trophies, one change of club).
- **Windows: built, not tested.** It uses the same reading code as FM27 Trainer, but the place of the match data
  on Windows has not been checked. If the app connects but finds no matches, please open an issue.
- Intel Macs: not tested.

### Known limits
- No goalscorers or player appearances yet.
- The table is computed from the results (points, goal difference, goals scored); leagues with other
  tie-breakers can differ.
- A final decided on penalties may be counted wrongly (not seen in testing).
- Squad and finance history starts when the app first sees your save.

On the Mac, FM27 has to start with a small module (*Start FM27* in the app). It is the same module FM27 Trainer
uses, so if you started FM with FM27 Trainer, Chronicle connects by itself.
